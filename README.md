PRIVACY POLICY FOR DESERTSKI
Last Updated: May 4, 2026

OVERVIEW

This privacy policy explains how the Desert Ski app handles your information. By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The app stores certain data locally on your device to improve your gameplay experience:
- High Score & Game Progress: saved locally so you can maintain your personal records and progression.
- Settings: your preferences.
This locally stored data remains on your device and is not directly transmitted to us.

THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING SERVICES

To support the continued development of DesertSki, the app uses third-party advertising networks to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. We use the LevelPlay mediation platform to serve ads from the following integrated networks:
- ironSource Mobile Ltd.
- Google AdMob
- Unity Ads

These third-party advertising partners may collect and process information about your device and gameplay to provide, measure, and optimize their services. The data collected by these third parties may include:
- Device identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google AAID)
- IP Address
- Device information (model, OS version, language, network type)
- App usage data and interactions with ads

This data may be used to serve personalized advertisements based on your interests or non-personalized advertisements based on contextual information.

For more information on how these ad networks handle your data, please review their respective privacy policies:
- ironSource: https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/air/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/
- Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

IN-APP PURCHASES

If the app offers in-app purchases, transactions are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We may also use services like RevenueCat to manage and sync your purchases. We do not process or store your payment details (such as credit card numbers).

USER CHOICES AND OPT-OUTS

You have control over how your data is used for personalized advertising:
- iOS Users: You will be prompted with an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) request asking for permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising. You can change your choice anytime in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Tracking).
- Android Users: You can opt out of personalized ads by navigating to your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads) and selecting "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID".

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our advertising partners may restrict the type of ads shown to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global laws. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information directly from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
